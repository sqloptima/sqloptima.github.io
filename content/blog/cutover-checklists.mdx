Migrations fail less from tooling and more from skipped rehearsal.

Baseline latency and top queries, rehearse cutover with schema compare gates, verify backups, and
keep a rollback owner. SQLOptima Migration and Monitoring exist to make that checklist measurable.

For SQL Server 2016 → 2022 engagements, use the phase-gated migration checklist and Compat 160
playbook in the handbook:

- [2016 → 2022 migration checklist](/handbook/sql-server-2016-to-2022-migration-checklist)
- [Post-migration Compat 160 tweaks](/handbook/sql-server-post-migration-compat160)
- [Interactive migration checklist](/knowledgebase/sql-server-2016-to-2022-migration-checklist.html)

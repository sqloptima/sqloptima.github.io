Dummy guide: Query Store regression views beat guesswork after a release.

Filter to queries whose average duration or CPU jumped across the change window, open the plan
history, and note whether the shape changed or only estimates drifted.

If the new plan is consistently worse, force the prior plan only after you understand *why* the
optimizer flipped — otherwise you paper over a stats or index problem that will return.

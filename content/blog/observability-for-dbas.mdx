---
title: Observability for DBAs is still SQL-shaped
description: OpenTelemetry helps, but wait stats still matter.
summary: Bridge app traces with database wait and plan evidence.
date: 2026-08-01
published: true
---

Distributed traces tell you which service slowed down. They do not explain latch contention.

Keep database-native telemetry first-class: waits, plans, IO stalls, and lock graphs. Then correlate
with traces when the path crosses app tiers.

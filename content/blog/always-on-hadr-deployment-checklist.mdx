Availability Groups fail more often in the middle of the stack than in the `CREATE AVAILABILITY GROUP` statement: DNS, quorum, endpoints, firewall rules, and restore sequencing.

Work Always On as a phase-gated build. Do not create the listener until secondaries are synchronized and failover has been tested in the change window.

## Order that reduces rework

1. Infrastructure, domain, accounts, DNS, and network
2. SQL install and Always On enablement
3. WSFC validate → create → quorum
4. Endpoints and firewall
5. Backup/restore to secondaries
6. AG, join, listener
7. Failover test, monitoring, documentation

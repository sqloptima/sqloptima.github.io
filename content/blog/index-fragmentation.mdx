Dummy guide: `avg_fragmentation_in_percent` alone will send you on rebuild hunts.

Filter to indexes with enough pages to matter, then ask whether the workload actually scans or
range-seeks that index. Point lookups on a fragmented B-tree rarely show up as user latency.

Measure logical fragmentation after large delete/update batches and after heavy autogrowth — those
are the moments fragmentation usually becomes real.

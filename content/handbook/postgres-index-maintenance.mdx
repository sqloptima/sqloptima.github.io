Watch for unused indexes, bloat estimates, and planner regressions after large deletes.

Prefer targeted `REINDEX CONCURRENTLY` on hot paths; avoid weekend-wide rebuild rituals.

Pair index work with vacuum discipline and realistic statistics targets.

```sql
SELECT schemaname, relname, idx_scan, idx_tup_read
FROM pg_stat_user_indexes
ORDER BY idx_scan ASC
LIMIT 20;
```
